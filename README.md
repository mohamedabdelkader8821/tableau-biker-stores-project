## Dashboard Questions
1. What are the overall revenue trends by year?
2. Which products generate the highest sales?
3. Which regions or categories perform best?
4. What are the monthly revenue trends?
5. Which customers or segments contribute the most revenue?

## Key Insights
1. 2017 generated the highest total revenue.
2. Mountain Bikes are the top-performing category.
3. Baldwin Bikes generates the majority of store revenue.
4. April 2018 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
5. Identified the most valuable customer segments.
